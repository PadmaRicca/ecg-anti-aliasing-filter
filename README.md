# Third-Order Butterworth ECG Anti-Aliasing Filter

**Project Level:** Advanced Undergraduate

**Institution:** Middlesex University London  
**Degree:** BEng (Hons) Biomedical Engineering  
**Module:** BMS3636 (Principles of Medical Electronics)  
**Academic Year:** 3rd (2024/2025)  
**Assessment Outcome:** First Class (Distinction)  
**Overall Module Mark:** 2 (First Class, Distinction)  
**Project Type:** Electronic circuit design, simulation, physical prototyping and printed circuit board (PCB) design project

## Project Overview

Designed, simulated and tested a third-order Butterworth low-pass filter for anti-aliasing in an electrocardiogram (ECG) recorder operating at a sampling frequency of 1,000 Hz.

The design targeted a cut-off frequency of approximately 150 Hz to preserve the relevant ECG frequency range while attenuating higher-frequency components before analogue-to-digital conversion.

The project combined mathematical analysis, electronic circuit simulation, physical prototyping and experimental validation, alongside PCB layout development. The PCB was designed but not physically manufactured or tested.

## Contributions

- Researched ECG signal characteristics, anti-aliasing requirements and relevant international standards to establish the circuit design criteria.
- Calculated component values and developed first-, second-, third- and fourth-order active filter circuits using NI Multisim, evaluating their frequency responses through AC sweep simulations.
- Constructed first-, second- and third-order filter prototypes using NI ELVIS breadboards and conducted experimental frequency-response testing through NI LabVIEW.
- Compared theoretical, simulated and experimental results, identifying approximately -24.85 dB attenuation at 500 Hz for the third-order prototype, against -25.39 dB in simulation.
- Designed a PCB layout and 3D visualisation for the third-order filter using NI Ultiboard, considering surface-mount component footprints, trace routing and relevant PCB design guidelines.
- Evaluated performance limitations associated with component tolerances, parasitic circuit effects and electromagnetic interference, proposing potential design improvements.

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Analogue Circuit Design:** Active filtering, operational amplifier configurations, frequency-domain analysis and component selection.
- **Circuit Simulation and Analysis:** Electronic circuit modelling, AC sweep analysis and interpretation of frequency-response characteristics.
- **Experimental Testing and Validation:** Electronic instrumentation, breadboard prototyping, Bode plot analysis and comparison of experimental and theoretical results.
- **PCB Design:** Surface-mount component selection, PCB layout development and three-dimensional board visualisation.
- **Engineering Problem-Solving:** Component tolerance analysis, evaluation of non-ideal circuit behaviour and critical assessment of design limitations.
